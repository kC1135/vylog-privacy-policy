VYLOG Privacy Policy

Open Spark Digital · Effective November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Open Spark Digital respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how VYLOG handles information when you use our vehicle maintenance and recordkeeping application.

VYLOG is designed to help users organize vehicle information, maintenance history, repairs, fuel purchases, reminders, receipts, and documents.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

VYLOG may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Vehicle details, including year, make, model, VIN, mileage, and photos
  • Maintenance and repair history
  • Fuel purchases and mileage records
  • Receipts, vehicle documents, and photographs
  • Maintenance reminders and preferences
  • Optional vehicle owner name
  • Backup and purchase-entitlement information
  • Your advertising consent choices, stored by Google's consent tool

VYLOG does not require users to create an account. Your core vehicle records are not automatically uploaded to servers operated by Open Spark Digital.

3. Information Shared With External Services

Certain optional features require communication with third-party services.

Vehicle identification and recalls: When you request VIN decoding, your VIN may be transmitted to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). Vehicle year, make, and model information may also be transmitted for recall lookups.

Vehicle specifications: When you request supported engine or fuel-economy information, vehicle year, make, and model information may be transmitted to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).

App updates: VYLOG uses Expo Updates, which may process technical information about your device, application version, and update requests.

Google Play services: The application includes Google Play Billing integration. Google Play may process app, product, and purchase-related information when billing features are used.

Purchase verification: When you buy or restore VYLOG Premium on Android, the app sends the Google Play purchase token, the app's package name, and the product ID to a verification service operated by Open Spark Digital on Google Cloud. The service checks the purchase with Google Play and acknowledges it. It does not receive your name, email address, or vehicle records, and it does not keep a database of purchases. Google Cloud may keep standard technical logs, such as request times and IP addresses, for a limited period.

App review access: A review access code is provided only to app store reviewers. If entered, it is sent to the same verification service to unlock Premium features for review.

Advertising: The free version of VYLOG shows banner ads from Google AdMob. See section 6.

These services operate under their respective privacy practices.

4. Photos, Camera, and Documents

VYLOG allows you to attach vehicle photographs, receipts, and documents. These files are stored locally in application-controlled storage. VYLOG does not automatically transmit your photographs or documents to Open Spark Digital servers.

5. Backups and Exports

VYLOG allows users to create local backups and export reports. You may choose to save or share exported files through other applications or services available on your device.

When you share an export with an external application, that service may process or store the selected information according to its own privacy policy. VYLOG does not currently provide automatic cloud backup to a developer-operated server.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of VYLOG shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob, a Google advertising service. VYLOG Premium users do not see ads, and the ad service is not started for them.

No personalized ads: VYLOG may be used by people aged 16 and 17, and it does not ask for anyone's age. So VYLOG treats every user conservatively: every ad request tells Google that the user may be under the age of consent, and ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens. Google does not show personalized ads or remarketing in VYLOG. VYLOG does not request access to your device's advertising ID.

What Google may collect: When ads are shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and operating-system details, app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and limit ads and to prevent fraud and abuse. Your vehicle records, photos, receipts, and documents are never shared with Google AdMob.

Your consent choices: VYLOG uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and U.S. states with applicable privacy laws. VYLOG does not load or request ads until this consent check is complete. Depending on your choices and where you are, you will see non-personalized or limited ads.

Changing your choices: You can review or change your advertising choices at any time in VYLOG under About → Privacy → Privacy Options. Where applicable, this includes withdrawing consent or opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Google's practices: Google processes this information under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

VYLOG does not use any other advertising networks or third-party analytics services.

7. Data Security

VYLOG uses device-local storage for its core records. The security of locally stored information also depends on your device's security settings and the protection of any exported backup files. Users should protect their devices and store exported backups securely.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Vehicle records remain locally stored until you delete them or remove the application's data. VYLOG provides controls for deleting vehicles, maintenance records, fuel logs, reminders, and attachments.

Uninstalling VYLOG or clearing its application storage generally removes locally stored application data. Files previously exported to other locations must be managed or deleted separately.

Information collected by Google for advertising is kept under Google's retention policies.

9. Children's Privacy

VYLOG is intended for general vehicle-management use and is not directed to children. Its ads are not intended for children. We do not knowingly operate a service for collecting personal information from children through VYLOG accounts.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published with an updated effective date.
